The view of the emulator is published.

  Reachable on the local network at
      http://{{ .Values.route.hostname }}{{ .Values.route.path }}/
      https://{{ .Values.route.hostname }}{{ .Values.route.path }}/

  User       {{ .Values.auth.user }}
  Password   in auth.local.yaml, not here

The https certificate is Traefik's built in default. The browser warns about
it, and rightly so: nobody signed it. On a local network that is acceptable,
facing the internet it would not be.

Check that the route stands:

  kubectl get ingressroute,svc,endpointslice -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  curl -u {{ .Values.auth.user }} http://{{ .Values.route.hostname }}{{ .Values.route.path }}/

The service behind it has to listen on {{ .Values.host.address }}:{{ .Values.host.port }}.
If nothing listens there, Traefik answers 502, and that is the right answer.
